The group records the membership fee against the fee year it has decided, and confirms your payment — until then your membership is not paid up, and only fully paid-up members are eligible for a farm assessment (OPP §2.3). Ask the administrator for the banking details and the reference to use.

What happens next

  1. The group reads what you have declared and comes back to you with any questions.
  2. Once the membership fee and the assessment fee are settled, a visit is scheduled — with at least five days' notice (OPP §4.1).
  3. The farmer questionnaire arrives before the visit. It is the work of the visit, not an exam: the assessors read it with you.
