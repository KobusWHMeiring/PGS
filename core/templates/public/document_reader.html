A citation opens the text we ingested, not a PDF page. The PDF is streamed as an attachment, so a `#page=` fragment has no viewer to land in; and the text is already in `DocumentChunk`, so a reader can *find* a quotation here in a way they can only hope for in a PDF viewer. One branch is rendered, not all of them: a 319-passage document becomes one section's markup, which is what makes the surface light.
