What the fees cover

Fees are set on a cost-recovery basis. They pay for the work of running a peer assessment group, not for a certificate.

The assessment fee covers

Administrative coordination, preparation, facilitation of the farm visit, reporting, the peer review process, and certification decision-making carried out by the PGS administrator.

OPP §2.2

When it is due

Membership fees are payable by the end of February each year, and the assessment fee is payable before the farm assessment visit.

The group pays the annual PGS South Africa membership fee out of the membership fees it collects.

Paying

Before you pay, ask a question

If you are not sure which band you are in, or whether a renewal applies, ask before paying. Renewal rates apply only where no significant changes to land, production system or inputs have occurred; where major changes are identified the group may require a full assessment at the initial rate.

OPP §2.3
