  • PGS South Africa — a member group, and the PGS control body The group pays the annual PGS South Africa membership out of the fees its members pay, and paid-up member groups are entitled to the “member of PGS SA” mark. SAOSO's own standard names PGS South Africa as the control body in the case of PGS initiatives. OPP §2.1 · Certification landscape p4 · SAOSO p24
  • SAOSO Standard v2.1 (2026) — the standard we assess against SAOSO's Standard for Organic Production and Processing “forms part of the IFOAM–Organics International family of standards”. Certification landscape p3
  • The PGS seals — a second-party guarantee with national standing PGS SA's own position is that its second-party seals “are as acceptable forms of organic certification, as any other type of certification, only the process for achieving the outcome of organic recognition is different.” Our committee may issue those seals under the outcomes the procedures define. Certification landscape p6 · OPP §V
  • Animal products — the DALRRD labelling rule Use of the word “organic” on animal products must be linked to protocols registered with the Department of Agriculture, Land Reform and Rural Development, and the IFOAM-recognised SAOSO standard covers those protocols. Certification landscape p6
