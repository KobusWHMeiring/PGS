{% comment %} The six membership steps, shared by the home page and /how-it-works/. The sequence is the design's (approved 2026-10-19) and follows OPP §I: sign the pledge (with the recorded-verbal-pledge provision for anyone who cannot read or sign the documentation), supply the information the group needs, familiarise yourself with the Basic Production Principles and the SAOSO standard — then the visit committee (OPP §3.3) and the committee's recorded decision (OPP §4). DESIGN §7.5 requires the recorded-verbal-pledge provision to appear; it is step 2. {% endcomment %}
  1. 1 Step 1

    Apply

    Approach the group as a prospective farmer or producer member.

    Initial contact

  2. 2 Step 2

    Pledge

    Sign the farmer member pledge. A recorded verbal pledge is accepted where reading is a barrier.

    Ethical agreement

  3. 3 Step 3

    Share

    Supply the group with the information it needs to assess your operation.

    Farm dossier

  4. 4 Step 4

    Prepare

    Familiarise yourself with the Basic Production Principles and the SAOSO standard.

    Self review

  5. 5 Step 5

    Visit

    A peer assessment team visits the farm and works through the assessment.

    Peer verification

  6. 6 Step 6

    Review

    The committee reviews the findings and records a decision.

    Formal record
