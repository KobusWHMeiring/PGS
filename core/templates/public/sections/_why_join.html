Assessed by people who farm

A farm visit is made by at least four people: the administrator, one consumer and two farmers. Not a hired inspector, and the person whose farm is being visited can see who is coming.

OPP §3.3

A decision, with the reasoning recorded

The committee records one of four outcomes against the SAOSO standard. Where findings need addressing, they are written down before the decision follows — so "not yet" always comes with what would change it.

OPP §4

Knowledge that moves between farms

Quarterly meetings, seed exchange and production problems solved by the people who have already met them. Members attend at least two other farm visits a year. This is the part of a PGS that never appears on a certificate.

OPP §3.2

A claim your customers can check

Independent retailers and markets join the same group and attend the visits from which they source. The people who sell your produce sit in the room where it is assessed.
