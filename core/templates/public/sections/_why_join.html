Assessed by people who farm

A farm visit is made by at least four people: the administrator, one consumer and two farmers. The person whose farm is being visited can see who is coming.

OPP §3.3

A decision, with the reasoning recorded

The committee records one of four outcomes against the SAOSO standard. Where something needs fixing, it is written down before the decision, so a “not yet” always says what would change it.

OPP §4

What members learn from each other

Members meet quarterly, exchange seed and bring production problems to people who have already met them. Everyone attends at least two other farm visits a year.

OPP §3.2

A claim your customers can check

Independent retailers and markets join the same group and attend the visits from which they source. The people who sell your produce sit in the room where it is assessed.
