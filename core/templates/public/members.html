Three kinds of member

Membership is annual and open to anyone in the group's catchment who works to the same principles — not only to farmers.

Farmer & producer members

Any farmer growing food organically along the Outeniqua mountain range — roughly from Mossel Bay to the Crags — is welcome to apply.

Farmer members sign the pledge, supply the information the group needs to assess them, and attend at least two other farm visits a year.

OPP §I, §3.2

Consumer members

Consumers join the same group on the same terms. They sit on farm visit committees — a visit has at least one consumer on it — and they take part in the quarterly meetings where the group's work is planned.

OPP §3.1, §3.3

Retailer & market members

Independent retailers and markets that source organic produce from the group's farms join as members and attend the visits from which they source.

OPP §3.1, §3.3

Membership is a requirement for endorsement

A farmer must be a paid-up member to be assessed, and only fully paid-up members are eligible for a farm assessment. Membership is a requirement for any farmer wishing to be PGS endorsed — but endorsement is a separate judgement, recorded by the committee after a visit.

OPP §2.3
