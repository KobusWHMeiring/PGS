{% comment %} A printed process, as cards (D77 E + C3, `LANDSCAPE_TABLES_PRD` §12f.2). Used only when the reviewed `` block declares `layout: process`. The data is the artefact's: `ReaderCard` carries the printed label, the reviewer's title, the step body and one facet per printed column; `ReaderFeeBox` carries a Fee cell and the cards it is printed under. Nothing here infers a step, a fork or a fee amount. Three rules the markup carries, and why: - **The step body leads.** The two text facets — Resources and *Use of seals* — fold (`
`, closed); a facet with no text is never folded, because a closed control with nothing inside is a dead control, so it keeps the sentence that the paper is silent. The picture column (`Seals and logos`) is not drawn at all: its cell is a caption for artwork the corpus does not store, so a control opening onto it would be a dead end (`§12i`). - **The fork is two cards in the last column.** Path A is a *pointer* to the step the paper's direct route branches from (§12f.5): it repeats no fee and states no outcome, because the only words it may add are UI ones ("Path A", "See …"). Path B, C … are the paper's own rows. - **A fee is a box under the card(s) it belongs to, and it is the whole cell** (D72.2). The merged cell on `ESTABLISHING A PGS GROUP` is one printed box across Steps 1–2; the span comes from the artefact's recorded empty cells, never from the legend's prose. {% endcomment %}
