Who comes to a farm visit

A visit is made by peers, not by a hired inspector — and the person whose farm is being visited can see exactly who is coming.

A farm visit committee

A minimum of four people: the administrator, one consumer and two farmers. The visited farm may also set a maximum number of additional participants who attend as observers without disrupting the assessment.

Farm visits are open to all members and non-members. Independent retailer and market members, or their representatives, are expected to attend all farm visits from where they source organic produce.

OPP §3.3

What a visit produces

The committee reviews the findings and records one of four decisions against the standard.

  • Certified organic — the operation and its listed products meet the standard for the next 12 months.
  • Organic — pending corrective action — findings are recorded and the decision follows their being addressed.
  • Organic in-conversion — the land or system is in transition, and the produce is recorded as in conversion rather than as organic.
  • Not endorsed / rescheduled — the assessment did not support endorsement, and the reason is recorded.

No farm status is published here

This release does not publish certificates, farm status or seals. A member list, when it is published, will say what membership is and is not — and it is not certification.
