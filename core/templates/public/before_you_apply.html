The page is a **stepped wizard with a no-JavaScript fallback**, and both shapes are deliberate: * With JS (`selfcheck.js`), the five groups are steps 1–5 with a numbered nav at the top, which is the shape the product owner approved on 2026-11-04 (§11.5). * Without JS, the same five sections render stacked with one submit button, because a farm connection is not a reason for an unreachable form. **No step gates the next on completeness**: the check is not a demand, and §3.5's "rather not say" already assumes silence is acceptable. The result is a **separate render** of the same route (§11.5's "one route, one view"): `result` and `stored` arrive from `evaluate()` and `submit()`, and every sentence in them comes from `core.services._selfcheck` — including the two fixed lines, which read the visit's own `MISSING_RECORD_BLOCKS_FINALISE` constant rather than being retyped here. The optional account at the end is A6's: `portal/_account_fields.html` and `AccountForm`, posting `step=account` to this same route.
