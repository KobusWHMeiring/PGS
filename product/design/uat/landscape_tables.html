How to use this page

This page does not check anything. It records your judgement - that is the whole point of a UAT item, and why the question comes before the steps.

  1. Run the preflight below. If a blocking row fails, stop and fix the instance.
  2. Fill in your name and role once, then use Use these details on every scenario.
  3. Run each scenario in the app, then choose PASS, FAIL or N/A. FAIL and N/A require a reason.
  4. Press Export sign-off and send the file to whoever writes it back.

Everything autosaves in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere: no network request is made by this page.

1

The fee question, which is why any of this exists

The judgement — the question you are answering

Did you find the answer without hunting, and does each box say what the money buys and who it is paid to? A box holding a bare "R500" is the failure D72.2 forbids — the amount is never published on its own. Say plainly whether you would quote this page to a farmer who asked.

Setup — do this first

the establishing a PGS group branch, read as a visitor would — from the top, not through the contents list.

Steps

  1. Read the three cards in order, down to the fee boxes. Ask the page the question the reader asked: what does it cost to join?
  2. Without scrolling back, say out loud what the joining fee covers and who it is paid to.
  3. Read Step 3's own fee the same way.

Expected

the two fees are in boxes under the steps they belong to; the joining box states the annual membership and the once-off amount inside the paper's sentence, and says it covers Steps 1–2; the certification fee sits under Step 3.

2

A card you can scan, and the one fold the design left to UAT

The judgement — the question you are answering

Is the step body now the thing that leads, and is anything you needed behind a fold? This is the decision the design deferred (§12f item 3) and the owner took it on 2026-11-03 (LANDSCAPE_TABLES_PRD §12i): Use of seals folds with the same element as Resources, because on Step 3 it is ten paragraphs and the facet that used to fold opened onto one caption, "Proud member of". Say whether that reads better than the open rule did.

Setup — do this first

the same branch. All three cards now fold their two text facets — Resources and Use of seals — closed by default. The Seals and logos column is not drawn: its cell is the printed caption of a picture the corpus does not store.

Steps

  1. Read the three cards as they arrive, judging the step body on its own.
  2. Open Resources, then Use of seals, on each step that has one. Read them.
  3. Notice what you had to open to answer a question, and what you never needed.

Expected

each card leads with the step and what happens in it; the reference material and the long rule are each one closed fold away, signed by the paper's own column heading; nothing is behind a fold that you did not want to open.

3

The fork reads as a fork, not as a fourth step

The judgement — the question you are answering

Would a reader understand they must take one route or the other — and that the pointer is a signpost, not a second summary of Step 2? The risk the design named: Path A drifting into a duplicate of Step 2, and repeating its fees (which D72.2 would then break in a new place). Judge whether it reads as a signpost at this size.

Setup — do this first

the SAOSO organic certification branch.

Steps

  1. Read Step 1 and Step 2 as steps.
  2. Read the third column top to bottom, through the or.
  3. Compare the pointer's wording with Step 2 — is it a signpost or a second summary?

Expected

the third column holds two cards stacked with an or between them; the first is a pointer to Step 2, the second is the paper's direct route with its own fee; the frames are dashed.

The comparison is a spreadsheet, and it reads without being dragged

The judgement — the question you are answering

Is the comparison legible without touching a control — and if you did resize, did the table end up more useful rather than merely different? A reader who never drags must still be able to compare the two seals. Say whether Compact is worth having at all.

Setup — do this first

the PGS seals and their use branch. First resize nothing.

Steps

  1. Compare the two seal rows across the five columns.
  2. Drag a column edge — or tab to a handle and use the arrow keys. Then reload the page.
  3. Press Compact, read the same rows, and press it again.

Expected

the header and the first column stay put as you compare; every column is the same type size; the resize handle is discoverable and does nothing destructive; Compact tightens the same columns rather than rearranging them.

6

What the pages refuse to claim

The judgement — the question you are answering

Would the committee be comfortable with these three pages as the group's public statement of what the landscape says? test_claim_discipline.py catches the forbidden terms and nothing about framing; the residue — a fee that reads as a quote, a route that reads as an endorsement — is this scenario.

Setup — do this first

all three branches, read for words rather than for structure.

Steps

  1. Read each branch and note every word the page adds that the paper does not print.
  2. Check what the pages say about the seals and about who is certified.
  3. Read the legend above each table.

Expected

the cards and fee boxes carry the paper's own words. The labels the renderer adds are neutral — Steps 1–2, Step 3, Path A, Path B — and the legend says the seals are pictures that are not stored. No page says a farm, a person or a product is certified.
