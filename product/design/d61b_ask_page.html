Questions we have answered

6 questions

Do I need to know where my horse manure comes from?

Yes — the source decides the rule. Extensive livestock farming is fine; intensive or conventional needs permission and composting; factory-farming origin is forbidden.

Animals SAOSO · Appendix 2 · p.76 asked 6 times

Can I use cardboard from my neighbour?

The standard does not name cardboard. It governs it as a biodegradable by-product: free of significant contaminants, or composted first.

Inputs SAOSO · Appendix 2 · p.76 asked 14 times

What is the yearly cost to get certified, including the farm visit?

Four amounts: R100 membership, the turnover-band assessment fee, catering per visit, and R500 a year to license the seal.

Money OPP · §2.1–§2.5 · pp.1–3 asked 21 times

Can I use Korean Natural Farming preparations?

The standard names them, with a limit: the ingredients must be among those listed in Appendix 2 and Appendix 3. The preparation’s name does not make it permitted — its ingredients do.

Inputs SAOSO · Appendix 3 · p.80 asked 4 times

If my ducks are not fed organic food and I compost their bedding, will I still pass?

Conditional: the bedding and the compost both have to satisfy Appendix 2, and the feed has to meet S5.5 — 5.5.1 requires that animals are fed organic feed.
