Get to know us

A guarantee system built on trust, not inspection

A Participatory Guarantee System is not an inspection regime. It is a flat group of peers who hold each other to a shared standard, and who learn from one another's farms. That is what makes it a PGS rather than a certificate bought from an agency.

The PGS

Guided by six basic elements

Six elements from the SA Organic Certification Landscape, which distinguish a participatory guarantee system from a paid certification agency.

[photo]

Shared Vision & Trust

Members share a common goal based on a shared need, and embrace the principles of PGS and organic agriculture. Trust and integrity form the ethical basis of the system.

[photo]

Horizontality & Transparency

A flat, equitable structure developed and managed by peers. All are responsible, all are accountable, and the system is open to scrutiny at all levels.

[photo]

Participatory & Learning

Members develop and manage the process collectively. Assessments focus on skills and knowledge exchange — traditional knowledge and innovation.

Membership procedure

How it works

Every prospective farmer and producer member follows the same path, in the same order, as set out in the group's Principles, Policies and Procedures.

01ApplyApproach the group as a prospective member.
02PledgeSign the farmer member pledge — verbal where reading is a barrier.
03ShareSupply what the group needs to assess your operation.
04PrepareRead the Basic Production Principles and the SAOSO standard.
05VisitA peer assessment team visits the farm.
06ReviewThe committee reviews and records a decision.

Membership is not certification

The people and farms listed here are members of the Outeniqua Natural and Organic PGS group. Membership of the group is not a certification, and does not by itself mean that a farm is certified organic. A certification decision is a separate judgement made by the group's certification committee against the SAOSO standard, and is recorded as such. Please do not read this list as a claim about any farm's organic status.
